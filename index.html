About Ayurhitham

A quieter kind of hospital, built around the patient's dosha

Ayurhitham was founded on a simple premise: Ayurveda works best when it isn't rushed. Our Vaidyas spend real time understanding your Prakriti before recommending a single therapy, and every treatment room is designed to be unhurried, private and calm.
